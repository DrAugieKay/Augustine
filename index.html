01 — About

Curious, rigorous, and impact-driven.

“I am a curious and keen student of all that goes on around me. I dislike confusion, inefficiency, half measures, and anything perceived as aimless or ineffective. I am tough when necessary, and open to cultural diversity.”

My work bridges management theory and data-driven practice: from stock-market development and FDI in Ghana, to post-merger conflict, supply-chain practices, microfinance inclusion, and banking competition. My doctoral work now explores how AI vs. managerial coaching frames shape employee self-efficacy.
